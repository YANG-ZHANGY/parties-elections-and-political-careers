# Policy commitments and coalition bargaining

[繁體中文](policy-commitments.zh-Hant.md) · [Theme overview](../README.md)

Concept note

## Research question

When do explicit policy commitments strengthen bargaining credibility, and when do they constrain coalition flexibility?

## The idea

This concept note considers the tension between credible promises and room for negotiation. It asks how parties use policy commitments as they seek to move from opposition into government.
