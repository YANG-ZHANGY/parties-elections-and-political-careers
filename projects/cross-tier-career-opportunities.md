# Cross-tier career opportunities

[繁體中文](cross-tier-career-opportunities.zh-Hant.md) · [Theme overview](../README.md)

Research overview

## Research question

How do opportunities at different political tiers enter the career choices of regional legislators?

## The idea

This research treats local, regional and national offices as connected opportunity structures. It examines how openings elsewhere in the political system relate to career movement, continued service and the organisation of party careers.

## Connections

This project belongs to [Parties, Elections and Political Careers](../README.md). It forms part of my broader research on comparative politics and international political economy.
