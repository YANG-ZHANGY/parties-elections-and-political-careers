Concept note

## Research question

Whose electoral signals do established parties follow when competitors gain ground?

## The idea

The research idea connects electoral clarity with the interpretation of challenger gains. It asks whether established parties respond to electoral competition along different policy dimensions, and how uncertainty about what voters signalled enters those responses.

## Connections

This project belongs to [Parties, Elections and Political Careers](../README.md). It forms part of my broader research on comparative politics and international political economy.
