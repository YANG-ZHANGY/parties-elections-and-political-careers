# Party institutions and legislative violence

[繁體中文](legislative-violence.zh-Hant.md) · [Theme overview](../README.md)

Research overview

## Research question

When does parliamentary competition escalate into physical confrontation?

## The idea

The project links the organisation of parties and party systems to the management of legislative conflict. It considers institutionalisation, the closure of political competition and polarisation as distinct features of the environment in which legislators confront one another.

## Connections

This project belongs to [Parties, Elections and Political Careers](../README.md). It forms part of my broader research on comparative politics and international political economy.
