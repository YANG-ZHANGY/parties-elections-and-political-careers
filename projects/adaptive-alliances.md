# Adaptive alliances and party continuity

[繁體中文](adaptive-alliances.zh-Hant.md) · [Theme overview](../README.md)

Concept note

## Research question

How can alliance strategies combine political adaptation with organisational continuity?

## The idea

This concept note considers alliance formation as a way for parties to respond to changing competition. It asks which organisational and electoral conditions make cooperation compatible with a continuing party identity.

## Connections

This project belongs to [Parties, Elections and Political Careers](../README.md). It forms part of my broader research on comparative politics and international political economy.
