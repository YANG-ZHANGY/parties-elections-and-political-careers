# Identity, cleavages and third-force voting in Taiwan

[繁體中文](taiwan-third-force-voting.zh-Hant.md) · [Theme overview](../README.md)

Research overview

## Research question

How do changing political cleavages shape the constituency of a third political force?

## The idea

This research examines how identities and political preferences combine in electoral choice. It considers the possibility that support for a familiar political position can be divided across different party options, making the composition of a third-force electorate central to understanding competition.

## Connections

This project belongs to [Parties, Elections and Political Careers](../README.md). It forms part of my broader research on comparative politics and international political economy.
