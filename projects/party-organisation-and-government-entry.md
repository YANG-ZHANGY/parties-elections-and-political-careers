# Party organisation and government entry

[繁體中文](party-organisation-and-government-entry.zh-Hant.md) · [Theme overview](../README.md)

Concept note

## Research question

How might the internal organisation of populist parties relate to their access to government?

## The idea

The concept note connects organisational structure with bargaining credibility, strategic coordination and coalition participation. It frames government entry as a relational political outcome involving both challengers and established parties.

## Connections

This project belongs to [Parties, Elections and Political Careers](../README.md). It forms part of my broader research on comparative politics and international political economy.
