# Candidate alignment and regional dominance

[繁體中文](candidate-alignment.zh-Hant.md) · [Theme overview](../README.md)

Research overview

## Research question

Why do candidates align with a party in one setting and maintain a different electoral position in another?

## The idea

The project connects regional party dominance with candidate alignment across political tiers. It distinguishes electoral labels from organisational ties and asks how local opportunities and broader party relationships enter strategic non-alignment.

## Connections

This project belongs to [Parties, Elections and Political Careers](../README.md). It forms part of my broader research on comparative politics and international political economy.
